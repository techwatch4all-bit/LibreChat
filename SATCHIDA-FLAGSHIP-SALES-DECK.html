Three structural failures of commercial cloud AI

Public AI subscriptions force enterprises to trade confidentiality, engineer hours and budget control for access to good models. None of the three is acceptable for regulated or IP-sensitive work.

1 · Identity & data bleed

Every prompt, code snippet and uploaded file is tied to a named corporate identity and logged on vendor infrastructure abroad. For NDAs, client mandates and the DPDP Act (2023), this is an exposure — not a feature.

Exposure: trade secrets, client data and employee identities sit on third-party logs.
2 · Fragmented toolchains

Engineers brief an AI in one tab, paste code into an editor, debug in a separate terminal, and hunt documents in a fourth window. Context is manually shuttled between tools all day.

Drag: industry estimates put the loss at up to 2.5 engineer-hours per day of copy-paste and context switching.
3 · The per-seat toll

₹1,700–₹2,500 per user per month, charged in USD to international cards with 3.5% forex markup plus GST, enforced per-user minimums, and usage throttled every few hours.

Toll: a 10-engineer team burns ₹3,00,000+ every year with nothing owned at the end.
