Supply of the Satchida AI for Teams workspace —
frontier AI for every desk at ₹249 a seat, with school-safe tutoring

1Executive summary

Satchida proposes the supply and support of a private AI workspace for your team and their families: frontier reasoning, a Knowledge Vault that answers with exact page citations, and Study mode tutoring — Socratic by design, POCSO-aligned for minors, with live usage oversight for parents. Pooled seats keep the effective cost at ₹249.75 per seat, billed in Rupees over NPCI UPI with GST invoicing.

2Scope of the offer
  • Private workspace — tenant at ai.satchida.com; seats per the commercial option selected; any phone, tablet or PC — no hardware, no installation.
  • Working modes — Chat and Work on every tier; Study mode (Socratic tutoring) from Family & Team upward. All modes run on frontier cloud providers.
  • Satchida Knowledge Vault — per-seat isolated document workspaces; answers cite document, page and paragraph; automated PPTX / PDF / DOCX / XLSX generation.
  • Study mode safety — Socratic pedagogy (hints and step-checks, never finished homework), POCSO Act (2012)-aligned real-time filtering, clean audio read-aloud; where study material is loaded into the vault, answers cite it page by page.
  • Pooled seats & oversight — one shared usage budget flexing across member accounts; pooled usage visible to parents and administrators — private chats are never exposed.

8Data protection, safety & compliance
  • Per-seat isolation: each member's documents live in their own vault workspace — commingling is structurally impossible.
  • Child safety: POCSO Act (2012)-aligned real-time filtering on Study mode; read-aloud strips internal reasoning before speech.
  • Parent & admin oversight: pooled usage visible; private conversations are never exposed.
  • Cited answers: vault responses carry document, page and paragraph — verifiable, not paraphrase.
  • Data portability: export and deletion of your data from the dashboard at any time; no annual lock-in.
  • DPDP Act (2023): workspace architecture designed for Indian data-protection compliance.
