Industry Context

The Three Critical Failures of Commercial Cloud AI

Public cloud AI vendors force enterprises to trade away confidentiality, budget sanity, and workflow efficiency.

1. Identity & Data Bleed

Public cloud subscriptions tie every prompt, file, and code snippet directly to your corporate identity and employee accounts. Prompts and telemetry are logged on remote servers, violating privacy standards under India's DPDP Act (2023) and NDA mandates.

Vulnerability: Exposure of trade secrets, intellectual property, and client data.
2. Disconnected Toolchains

Developers constantly waste time context-switching: copying prompts into chat tabs, pasting generated code into editors, debugging in separate terminals, and searching external docs. Over 2.5 hours per engineer are lost every day in manual back-and-forth.

Friction: Fragmented context, constant manual copy-pasting, and workflow drag.
3. Inflated Seat Taxes

Standard big tech subscriptions demand $30 to $60/user/month in foreign currency with 3.5% foreign exchange charges, rigid per-user minimums, and strict throttling limits that punish collaborative team usage.

Cost Toll: A small 10-person engineering team burns over ₹3,00,000 every year.
