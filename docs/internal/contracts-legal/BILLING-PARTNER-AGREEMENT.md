## 1. What the Agent Does

The Agent:
- Issues GST-compliant tax invoices to Clients of the Principal's software licence business
- Collects payment (including applicable GST) from those Clients
- Remits the net amount to the Principal after deducting commission per clause 3

The Agent acts on the Principal's instructions for each invoice. The Agent has no authority
to vary any terms of the Principal's software licence, negotiate on the Principal's behalf,
or make any representation about the Principal's software or service.

---

## 2. What the Agent Does NOT Do

The Agent has **no role whatsoever** in:
- Providing, supplying, operating, maintaining, or supporting any software or technology
- Accessing, storing, or processing any data belonging to any client or end user
- Making any decision about the Principal's business, pricing, or clients
- Representing the Principal in any legal, regulatory, or technical matter

The Agent is a billing intermediary **only**.

---

## 3. Commission

The Agent retains **2% (two percent)** of each net collected invoice amount as commission.

"Net collected amount" means the amount received from the Client, excluding GST. GST
collected belongs to the government and is remitted by the Agent directly.

The Agent remits the balance to the Principal within **[7] business days** of the
payment clearing in the Agent's account. Payment via NEFT/IMPS/UPI to the Principal's
account as directed.

If a client pays late, the commission clock starts from actual receipt, not invoice date.

---

## 4. GST and Tax

The Agent invoices under the Agent's own GSTIN. The Agent files and remits GST collected.
The Agent issues the Principal a statement of collections and remittances each month.

The Principal is responsible for income tax on all amounts received from the Agent.
TDS, if applicable, is deducted by the Agent per the applicable rate and the TDS certificate
is issued to the Principal within the statutory period.

Neither party is responsible for the other's tax obligations beyond what this clause says.

## 5. Complete Legal Immunity of the Agent — The Core Clause

The Agent's role is billing only. The Agent has no knowledge of, control over, or
responsibility for:
- The Principal's software or technology
- Any service provided to any client or end user
- Any data processed, stored, or transmitted by the Principal's system
- Cloudflare, any ISP, any cloud provider, or any technical infrastructure

**Therefore:**

> **5.1** The Agent has **no liability whatsoever** to any Client, end user, regulator, ISP,
> cloud provider, government body, court, or any other party for any claim, loss, damage,
> fine, penalty, or cost arising from the Principal's software, the Principal's service, the
> Principal's business, or any act or omission of the Principal.

> **5.2** If any claim, demand, legal proceeding, regulatory inquiry, or enforcement action
> is brought against the Agent arising from the Principal's software, service, or business
> — whether by a client, an end user, an ISP, Cloudflare, a regulator, or any third party —
> the Principal shall **immediately, at the Principal's own expense**:
> (a) assume full conduct of the defence of that claim;
> (b) indemnify and hold harmless the Agent from all liability, loss, cost, damage, and
>     expense (including legal fees on a full indemnity basis) arising from or connected
>     with that claim;
> (c) ensure the Agent is removed as a named party as soon as legally possible.

> **5.3** This immunity applies to claims of every kind — contract, tort, negligence,
> statutory breach, regulatory, criminal — whether or not the Agent was aware of the
> underlying facts. The only exception is the Agent's own deliberate fraud.

> **5.4 Cloudflare and ISPs specifically.** Any claim arising from Cloudflare's handling
> of the Principal's traffic, any ISP's routing of the Principal's data, or any other
> infrastructure component used by the Principal is entirely the Principal's matter.
> The Agent has no relationship with those parties and no liability arising from them.

## 6. Mutual Immunity from Client Disputes

If a Client disputes an invoice for reasons related to the Principal's software or service
(not a billing error), the Principal handles that dispute. The Agent forwards the dispute
to the Principal and takes no further action until instructed.

If the dispute is a genuine billing error by the Agent (wrong amount, missed credit), the
Agent corrects it and remits any shortfall. The Agent's liability for billing errors is
limited to the correction — no consequential loss.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

Both parties keep the following confidential:
- The names and contact details of all Clients
- Invoice amounts and payment history
- The terms of this agreement
- Any information about the Principal's business shared in confidence

Neither party discloses these without the other's consent, except as required by a court
order, tax authority, or regulator — and even then, only to the extent required.

---

## 8. Duration and Exit

Either party may end this arrangement with **30 days' notice** by any traceable written
means (email or WhatsApp with read receipt is sufficient).

On exit: the Agent completes all pending invoices for work done before the notice date,
remits the final balances, and transfers any client account information to the Principal.
No penalty applies to either party for ending the arrangement.

---

## 9. Relationship of Parties

This agreement creates a billing agency only. It does not create:
- A partnership
- A joint venture
- An employment relationship
- A shareholding or equity interest
- Any authority to bind the other party to any third-party agreement
