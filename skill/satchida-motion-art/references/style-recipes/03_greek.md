# 03_greek · 530 BC Attic Black-Figure Pottery — Code Technique Notes

File: `scenes/03_greek.js` (pure Canvas 2D, no images). Final self-check: warm max 15.9ms / mean 7.6ms / cold 80ms, determinism passes, motion diff at 0.2↔0.7s has 22% of pixels moving.

## One-line trick
Black-figure = **black-glaze silhouette + incised lines + added color**, all three required:
- Silhouette: all figures/objects filled `#1b120d` (near-black with warmth), no stroke, formed by silhouette against the terracotta ground.
- Incised lines: "carved through" the black silhouette, drawn as 1.5–2px terracotta bright-orange `#e8904c` thin lines (drapery folds, facial features, boundaries where limbs overlap).
- Added color: purple-red `#8a2a37` bands (headband, cuffs, hem, collar, cushion) + small white dots (`#f6ead6`, r≈2, spacing 11–17px).
Do these three and it reads as a Greek vase at a glance without any texture.

## Palette
Terracotta `#dc6a2f`, bright terracotta (vase panel) `#e8803f`, black glaze `#1b120d`, purple-red `#8a2a37`, white `#f6ead6`, incised lines `#e8904c`, blond hair `#f2bd48`, terracotta reserve (cat's white areas) `#f3cfa4`, cat orange `#f2913f`, cat nose `#e7897a`.

## Pipeline and parameters
1. **Terracotta ground (cached `gr_bg`)**: computed per-pixel at 480×270 low resolution — fbm(×0.016, 4 octave) mottling ±30 + **horizontal band noise** `noise(x*0.006, y*0.85)` (wheel-throwing striations) ±9, upscaled to 1920×1080 (low frequencies are blurry anyway, saving 16× computation); then 560 random horizontal fine lines (length 150–1050, alpha 0.03–0.08, dark/light variants) as wheel fine lines; 420 dark specks + 120 light specks; two `P.grain` layers; 5 cracks (segments every 9px, ±2px jitter, plus a 1px-offset pale bright edge below — cracks instantly gain thickness). A warm radial brightening of 0.16 stacked at center. Cold start ~80ms for the full frame.
2. **Vignette (cached `gr_vig`)**: radial 380→1180, outer edge rgba(30,6,0,.62), topmost layer over the whole frame (including the ornamental bands).
3. **Static black-figure layer (cached `gr_static`)**: picture frame (black border 18px + white dot string at 11.6px spacing + inner fine line), grapevine (sine stem + triangular grape clusters in 3-2-1 dots), table (legs with clawed feet), lathe-turned chair legs (built symmetrically from a [y,w] width polyline), swan-neck chair back (13px heavy stroked curve + elliptical head + triangular beak), amphora (24-point symmetric outline → `RIG.smooth`, vase panel with terracotta reserve + small black cat, radiating lines at the foot, one vertical gloss gradient at 0.09), two passages of Greek inscription.
4. **Greek inscription**: the font library has no Greek glyphs → hand-wrote a **stroke table** (each letter 1–3 polylines, unit grid 0..1). Letter height 30, stroke width 3.4, each letter height ×(0.9–1.06) random, baseline jitter ±6%, vertex jitter ±0.7px; Ο drawn as a small circle (height 0.25, the original uses a small o), Ι 2px wide. The hand-painted vase-inscription flavor comes mainly from "unequal letter heights + small o".
5. **Scrolling ornamental bands**: the top meander (period 60px, 7px black lines, unit polyline `[[4,46],[4,0],[50,0],[50,34],[18,34],[18,14],[36,14],[36,22]]` + full-width baseline) and the bottom lotus-bud chain + ray pattern (period 76px = two 38px units, odd buds filled purple-red, so the true period is 76) are each pre-rendered into strips of width W+2P, each frame `drawImage(strip, -(off mod P))`. Offset curves:
   - Top: `off = 22·lt + 60·easeInOut((lt−0.49)/0.35)`, moving left;
   - Bottom: `off = −(18·lt + 76·easeInOut((lt−0.477)/0.367))`, moving right.
   The original only does one easeInOut period jump at frames 169–191; I additionally added an 18–22px/s constant drift so the first 0.47s of this segment is visibly moving too.
6. **Motifs inside the frame** (drawn each frame, clipped into the frame): 6 dolphins (hand-written bezier silhouettes: beak, dorsal fin, flipper, tail fork + belly incised line + white eye dot) swimming back and forth ±9px along their own heading, angle ±0.12rad, period 0.85s, phases offset by 1.3; the boat rocks around (560,420) ±4px and tilts ±0.025rad, period 0.9s. White sail + purple-red stitching, crescent hull + scorpion-tail stern, purple-red cabin, reclining figure.
7. **Lyre**: swinging on the nail (1050,150) as axis, `0.09·sin(2π·lt/0.8+0.5)` (±5°, displacement at the soundbox ±17px, clearly visible within a second). Tortoise-shell soundbox = black circle + incised ring + 5 small incised circles.
8. **Rosette**: 8 black petal ellipses + incised petal divisions + purple-red center, rotating at `lt·1.6` rad/s alternating direction.
9. **Orange-white cat** (hand-drawn, not the RIG default polygons; tail uses `RIG.cat` tailPts swinging with choreo.tail): whole cat scaled 0.9 into the standard frame anchored at (548,903); orange silhouette + black outline 3.4px; white chest/paws/muzzle in the "terracotta reserve" cream; **back stripes: take 12 arc-length-equispaced points along the back outline, draw tapered stripes toward the body center (575,800)** (length 30–48, root width 10) — far more natural than hand-placed stripes; thigh arcs, vertical foreleg stripes; archaic big round eyes (white eye + black pupil + highlight + a brow arc above), blinking swaps to an arc; purple-red collar with white dots.
10. **Girl**: `RIG.girl({cup,sip,breathe,s:0.97,dy:-14,hair:'long'})` — scaled and shifted so feet land on the ground line 903.
    - Body filled directly from the RIG Path2Ds (farSleeve/skirt/torso/neck); far hand replaced with a small ellipse (RIG's farHand reads as a blade inside the black silhouette).
    - **Head hand-drawn**: a Greek profile (straight nose bridge joining the forehead, fine chin) + hairstyle outline written in RIG's rest coordinate system, replicating the RIG's head motion with a canvas transform: `translate(neck)·rotate(G.tilt)·translate(−neck)·T`. The same matrix computed again via `DOMMatrix`, `Path2D.addPath(faceSil, M)` used as the incised-line clip region.
    - **Blond hair** (the key to recognizing the "blond girl"): inside the hair-region clip draw 11 top-of-head wavy strands (quadratic bezier + normal-direction 2.6px·sin(7πu) waviness), 8 locks of long back curls (sine 4.5 half-waves + small end spiral), double-spiral bun, 4 small forelock curls, two spiral sidelocks (black 6px backing then gold 2.2px on top, readable even crossing outside the silhouette). Gold lines need 2.5px at 1080p to have enough "mass"; at 2px they vanish in thumbnails.
    - Incised lines: 16 drapery folds (clipped inside the body), almond eyes (pale sclera + black pupil, frontal eye on the profile face), brows, nostril wing, lips, chin. **Boundaries where the near arm overlaps body/head**: clip(body ∪ head) then stroke the arm outline's incised lines — black-on-black can only be separated this way, or the face smears when the hand rises to the mouth.
    - Added color: 15px purple-red hem band + white dots, cuff band (clipped inside the upper arm, drawn along the arm's normal as a 15px band + incised lines above/below), bracelets, 8 white-dot four-petal flowers, headband.
    - Kylix cup (wide and shallow with two handles, half-width 32) follows `G.cup` (including tilt), purple-red rim line; steam is three "≀" black fine lines, phase `t·9`.

## What moves (all visible within 0–0.94s)
Cup drinking (choreo.cup raises to the mouth over 0.9s + slight head tilt), cat tail/blink/breath, top and bottom band scrolling (drift + beat-period jumps), lyre swing, boat bobbing, dolphins swimming, rosette turning, steam twisting.

## Pitfalls hit
- The RIG hair outline scaled up directly is too wide at the back of the head (v1 reached x=1450, head like a helmet); trimmed to 1424 per the design sheet, long hair only down to y≈558.
- With the cup at the mouth, black hand + black cup + black face smear into one: (1) incised-line separation of arm against head, (2) cup shrunk (half-width 44→32), (3) a purple-red line left at the rim.
- The RIG cat's head top reaches y=535; the standard frame's cat top is 610. Without scaling it presses on the picture frame's bottom edge.
- The bottom band's alternating odd/even buds make the true period 2 units (76px); the pre-rendered strip's wrap period must use 76, otherwise the color jumps every 38px.
- `P.grain`'s seed = key length; the two grain layers' keys must differ in length, otherwise the patterns are identical.
- **The cup-drinking endpoint must be period-appropriate (review fix)**: the RIG's cup→mouth endpoint sits near the nose tip with the cup tilted −0.45rad; swapped for the wide shallow kylix it reads as a bird beak/pipe stuck in the face, with the hand covering mouth and chin. Rebuilt with own IK: lip point rest(1243,391) transformed to screen by the head's DOMMatrix, rim center = lip point −(30,−1) (rim's right end just touching the lips), tilt −0.08·cup (≤5°), hand IK target = rim +(lerp(32,6,cup), 30), i.e. cupped under the stem; `RIG.ik2(S, handTarget, 165s, 122s, −1)` + `RIG.taper` produce upper arm/forearm. The forearm stands in front of the chest; the profile contours of nose, lips, and chin stay fully visible. Steam follows the rim.

## v1 review fixes (cat converted to black-figure + top band scroll gain)
- **The cat must use the same drawing system as the person**: a bright-orange big-round-eyed cartoon cat pops out of black-figure instantly. Converted to a black-glaze silhouette, with "orange-white" carried only by added color and incised-line zoning: along the back a 26px purple-red saddle band, a purple-red patch on the head (ellipse 46×22 clipped inside the head), tail black with a 7px purple-red core; all tabby marks are terracotta-orange `#f2913f` 3px **incised lines** (14 curved lines equispaced along the back outline toward the body center + 3 on thighs + 3 on forelegs + a ring every 2 tail points); white chest/muzzle/paws in white pigment `#efe1c8` with black incised toes; eyes changed to archaic incised almond eyes (white-pigment sclera + vertical black elliptical pupil + a small incised flick at the eye corner), a blink is one downward-curved incised line; nose purple-red, whiskers incised on the left (falling on the black face), black lines on the right (falling on the terracotta).
- **Scrolling a periodic pattern by whole periods = first and last frames identical**: after one 60px period jump of the top band the frame is exactly the same as before the jump; in static comparison or sampled frames it's easily judged "not moving". Constant drift raised 22→48px/s (bottom band 18→30px/s) to guarantee phase difference between any two frames.
