# 16_2026 · 2026 flat vector illustration + the whole film's comedy finale

All performance is written with **whole-film frame numbers `F = t*60`**; key numbers copied line by line from `../storyboard/storyboard.md` §4 (the storyboard sheet), for frame-by-frame comparison against the original.
Contact sheet (left original | right replica): `../renders/16_2026/cmp_E1…E4_*.jpg`, clips `clip_2026.mp4` (10.8–15.06s).

## Style (Dribbble flat + grain)
- Pure color blocks, no outlines; volume is separated only by "blocks one shade darker in the same family" (sweater lit-side diagonal block `#f08b7d`, cat thigh `#f3ad4c` + one dark arc edge).
- **Grain stipple `stipple()`**: 2px square dots scattered inside shapes, density a function of position — pink circle `0.12 + 0.75·clamp((y-cy+120)/420)` (denser downward), pale-lilac block denser toward lower-right, sun denser toward lower-right. All static parts go into cache `fl_bg`.
- After the character layer is drawn, one static grain texture is composited on with `source-atop` (5% dark dots α70, 3.5% bright dots α90) — grain falls only on the characters.
- Light cone: 26000 points, width linear 56→150 with y, opacity decaying with y, denser near center, cached as a 340×420 sprite, swinging with the pendant lamp around (1010,0).

## Girl (custom poses on the RIG base)
- Torso/neck anchors: `RIG.girl({s:1.12})`; the head scaled again with `RIG.girl({s:1.344, dy:43.7})` — equivalent to the head magnified 1.2× around the neck point, the flat-illustration big-head ratio. Face, fringe, back hair, bun, eye/lip/ear anchors all taken from it.
- Arms: `RIG.ik2(shoulder, target, 150, 140, -1)`, three target sets blended by progress: typing (both hands on keyboard alternating up/down 7px at 22 rad/s) → petting cat (688–704, near hand drawing small circles on the cat's back) → exclaim (791–798, `ease.outBack` raised in front of the chin, fingers spread).
- Expressions: closed-eye arcs + upturned mouth (<686) → dot eyes + highlight, small-o mouth (from 686, the o popping with outBack) → big eyes + wide-open mouth + tongue (from 791) → after frame 838 squeezed "＜" eyes (the original's final frames are closed too).
- Stress lines: 3, radiating from above-front of the head, length popping with outBack; color 793 pale pink → 796 full red (RGB interpolation); 830–888 random jitter ±2.5px every 2 frames, head jittering ±2px in sync.
- Pant legs/socks/shoes hand-drawn (the RIG is a long dress).

## Cat (pose library + interpolation)
Each pose is a set of local keypoints (facing right, origin = centroid): body 10 points, white chest 6 points, thigh ellipse, back stripes 3 segments, head, two forelegs, hind leg, tail 4 bezier points, gy (paw base to centroid).
Poses: `SIT_F` (sitting on the floor, measured from the original at 10.95s), `SIT_T` (sitting on the table, measured facing left in the original then mirrored to face right for storage), `LEAP` (horizontally stretched), `LAND` (forepaws down). `mixPose` lerps per value.
Transform: `translate(centroid) → [around paw base or around centroid] rotate → scale(dir·sx, sy)`; squash and stretch pivot on the paw base, so the feet stay grounded while crouching.

## Cup and shattering
- Cup x: linear interpolation of 16 measured points from the storyboard (744:981 … 796:801); 790–796 starts tipping outward -0.12.
- Fall 796–815: 9 measured centers via Catmull-Rom (measured gravity ≈1.75px/frame²), rotation `-0.12 - 2.3·q^1.3`; 6 coffee drops behind it take the cup's position at 1.3-frame intervals, smaller the earlier.
- 815 landing: 7 impact radiating lines fading over 8 frames; two large shards use the storyboard's three points (818/824/834) through Lagrange parabolas, stopping at the ground at 834; 9 small shards on parabolas, each stopping at its own ground height 893–911; coffee stain width 818:140 → 822:220 → 826:235 (ease.out); two gray ripples expanding to 560/460 wide, fading before 838; handle half-disc + tea-bag tag left on the ground.
- Slap motion lines: 3 short lines right of the cup, expanding and fading over 12 frames after impact.

## Environmental secondary motion
- Pendant lamp: `damped(F, 686, 0.055rad, period 26 frames, decay 22 frames) + damped(F, 830, 0.045, 22, 20)`, light cone swinging with it.
- Plant: `damped(F, 697, 0.035, 22, 18)` (sway) + `damped(F, 821, 0.05, 9, 26)` (jolt, short period); each monstera leaf adds a bit of self-sway with different phase.
- Clouds drifting left 15px/s (0.25px/frame); small plane 177px/s with dashed trail (x≈440 at 12.0s, x≈750 at 13.75s, matching the original); sun rays rotating 0.35 rad/s; 17 decorative symbols boiling at 4fps (±1.5px, ±0.09rad).
